Hi! I'm Renato, Product Designer with 20+ years of experience, most of it inside startups, early-stage products, and new launches. I've worked across finance at C6 Bank, Portocred, Itaú, and XP Investimentos, e-commerce at Mobly (Rocket Internet), and most recently AI-native products at Superside.

Throughout my career, I've developed a practical sense for balancing speed with design process — a skill built from years spent in diverse environments that each demanded a different approach.

I believe in collaborative environments with close proximity between Design, Strategy, and Technology — the kind that ensures both users and business goals actually get met. More recently, I've been incorporating AI into that approach, using it to move faster, get to better, more refined outcomes, and bring teams closer around ideas, research data, and possible solutions.
